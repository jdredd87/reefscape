Reefscape Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Reefscape is a screensaver for Roku devices created by Steven Chesser. This policy explains what information it handles.

Information we collect

None. Reefscape does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Settings stored on your device

The choices you make in Reefscape's settings (lighting mode, clock display and which animals appear) are saved only on your own Roku device so they are remembered next time. They never leave the device. Removing Reefscape from your Roku deletes them.

Roku

Roku, Inc. operates the Roku platform and Streaming Store and may collect information under its own privacy policy, which Reefscape does not control. See roku.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Reefscape collects no information from anyone, including children.
