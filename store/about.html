Reefscape for Roku

Turn your TV into a window on a living coral reef. Reefscape is a free, richly animated underwater screensaver for Roku devices.

What you'll see

More than 15 kinds of sea life share the reef: blue and yellow tangs, angelfish, a clownfish pair in their anemone, schools of silver fish, lionfish, reef sharks, sea turtles, manta rays, glowing jellyfish, an octopus, a pod of dolphins, a distant humpback whale, seahorses, crabs, sea stars, a moray eel peeking from its cave and a snapping shrimp popping out of the sponges. Now and then a scuba diver drifts by, or a baited fishing hook comes down, but the fish always steal the worm.

The reef follows your Roku's clock: warm light at sunrise and sunset, and at night the water darkens while plankton and jellyfish glow.

How to turn it on

  1. Add Reefscape from the Roku Streaming Store.
  2. On your Roku go to Settings > Theme > Screensavers and choose Reefscape.
  3. To change options, choose Custom settings.

Settings

  • Lighting: follow the clock, always day, always sunset, always night, or a fast day/night cycle.
  • Clock: off, time, date, or date and time. It moves around the screen to help prevent burn-in.
  • Animals: choose which animals appear.

Free and private

No account, no ads, no internet connection needed, and no data collected.
